- STYLE OF VIRAL INDIAN RECIPE SHORTS (before writing, web-search the most-viewed Hindi recipe Shorts this month and for this dish, and copy their FORMAT, never their content): food is the star; lots of tight close-ups and overhead (top-down) shots into the kadai/tawa/plate; every ingredient visibly drops/pours in with a satisfying sound (pop/plop cue + sizzle cue on hot oil, bubbles cue for boiling); tadka crackle close-up; steam rising; cheese/ghee/butter melt moments; quick cuts every 1-2 s; Didi appears for the hook, a few steps (stirring, tasting with a spoon, "mmm!" nod) and the end. STRUCTURE: (1) HOOK 0-2 s: open on the most mouth-watering picture (finished dish steaming, tadka crackling in the kadai) + a hook line ≤ 8 words ("10 minute mein khile-khile poha!"); (2) INGREDIENTS ~4-5 s: each ingredient pops in as a Roman pill with its quantity ("Poha · 2 cups"); (3) 4-6 STEPS: a step pill ("STEP 2") + quantity/time overlay, Didi doing the action at the stove, camera always moving, new shot every 1-2 s; (4) SERVE + CTA: plated dish, Didi smiles at camera, "aur aisi recipes ke liye subscribe karo!", last line loops back to the hook.
